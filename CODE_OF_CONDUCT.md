# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is acting in good faith and is
better informed than you about their own context.

## What is expected

- Criticise the code, the schema or the argument. Not the person.
- Take disagreement about technical decisions as normal. This project declines
  features on purpose and explains why; pushing back on that reasoning is
  welcome, and doing it with evidence is welcome most of all.
- Assume a question is a real question. Someone asking why absence and null are
  separate is not wasting your time, they are the reason the documentation
  exists.
- Accept a "no" without escalating. Scope is a design decision, not a
  negotiation to be reopened in a new thread.

## What is not

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone rather than their argument.
- Harassment of any kind, in the open or in private, including unwanted contact
  after being asked to stop.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to wear people down,
  derailing threads, or bad-faith argument.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project occupies: issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, and any private contact that starts from one of
them. It also applies when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report it privately to the maintainer through GitHub, by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/angeldevmobile/Seam/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repository has. Say what happened and
link the thread if there is one.

Seam is maintained by one person. You will get an acknowledgement that the
report was read. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so plainly in it;
that is a fair thing to report and it will be treated as one.

Reports stay private. The reporter's identity is not shared without their
consent.

## Consequences

What happens depends on what occurred and whether it continues. In order:
a private word, a public correction, removal of the offending content, a
temporary block, a permanent one.

The maintainer decides, explains the decision to the person affected, and lives
with having got it wrong sometimes.
